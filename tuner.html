      Good to know

        New to tuners? How to read this one
        • Play one note at a time and let it ring. The big letter is the note it hears.
        • The needle shows how far off you are. Left of the middle = too low (called “flat”). Right = too high (“sharp”).
        • Adjust until the needle sits in the middle and everything turns green. Within 5 “cents” is in tune. A cent is 1/100 of a note.
        • Tighter string = higher note. Looser = lower. If you’re not sure which way a peg goes, turn it a little and watch the needle.
        • Leave the reference pitch on 440 Hz unless you’re matching something else (like an old piano).
        Using this on an iPhone
        • Open the link in Safari. Tap Start tuning, then Allow when it asks for the microphone.
        • If you tapped “Don’t Allow” by mistake, tap aA in the address bar, then Website Settings › Microphone › Allow, and reload.
        • To keep it like an app: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
        • No sound from “Hear it”? Check the volume, and flip the silent switch on the side of the phone off.
        • The tuner stops when you switch apps or lock the phone. Tap Start again when you come back.
